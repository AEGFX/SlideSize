VT Inspector

Drop one or more MP4, MOV, M4V or M4A files to inspect the bitstream for anything that will stutter, stretch, or fail to play on a show machine. Catches misdeclared H.264 levels (a classic Windows PowerPoint stutter), VFR frame rates, odd pixel aspect, bit depth, interlacing, HDR colour tags, edit-list trims, non-faststart layout, and more. Where there is a fix, you get the ffmpeg command, and for the common level-mismatch case you get a two-byte header rewrite you can download right here with no re-encode and no quality loss.

The rules change by target. Windows PowerPoint is the strictest, catches things macOS plays fine.
Flags files whose rate does not match the project (24 into 25 and similar).
