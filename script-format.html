Script Format

Drop or paste a script and get every sentence on its own line, ready for the prompter. It reads each full stop in context, so U.S.A., Dr. Patel, 9 a.m., e.g. and J. K. Rowling stay in one piece. Calls it isn't sure about are flagged amber, and any break can be added or removed with a click. Copy it, or save as TXT or Word. Word files keep their highlights, colours and bold.
