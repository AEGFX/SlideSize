PDF to Slides PDF → PNG + PPTX

Drop a PDF and get back a folder of numbered images plus a PowerPoint deck with one image per slide, sized to match the PDF rather than squashed into a guess. Useful when a client sends artwork as a PDF and the show file needs slides. Nothing is uploaded, it all runs here in the browser.
