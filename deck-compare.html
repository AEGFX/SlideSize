Deck Compare

Drop two versions of the same .pptx and get a slide by slide list of what changed. Slides are tracked by PowerPoint's own slide IDs, so a slide that moved from 8 to 11 is still recognised as the same slide. Catches text edits word by word, swapped pictures and videos, playback settings, hidden slides, transitions, notes, fonts, moved, added and deleted slides. Export to TXT, CSV or print to PDF.
