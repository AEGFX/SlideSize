Drop a .pptx and see every embedded asset as a thumbnail. The filenames inside a deck are flat and non-descriptive (image1.png, image47.jpg) so this gives you a visual way to find the one you need. Click any tile for its metadata and a per-file save, or save the lot into a folder of your choice. Nothing is uploaded, it all runs in your browser.
