EMF Extractor

Mac PowerPoint and other Mac apps often save EMF graphics with a full-quality PDF hidden inside, inside an Office GDIC comment block. Windows PowerPoint only draws the tiny fallback bitmap and ignores the PDF, which is why those graphics render blurry when a Mac deck is opened on a Windows show machine. This pulls the real PDF (or the fallback raster) back out. Drop a single .emf, or a .pptx to list every EMF inside the deck.
